SUMMARY: Nine non-DB reflex-examples apps were upgraded in place from 0.10.1 to 0.11.0a1: counter, github-stats, overkey, reflexle, upload, clock, quiz, snakegame and lorem-stream. Each upgrade kept the same venv, .web, reflex.lock and browser profile, and each app was driven in Chromium in dev, in prod and after a cold rebuild. Every app except overkey gives the same check results and console signatures as 0.10.1, and prints no new server-log messages. Overkey fails at import exactly as the sibling clusters reported. Only the five train packages move on an in-place upgrade without -U. lucide stays 1.1.1 and hosting-cli stays 0.2.1. With -U they move to 1.1.2a1 and 0.2.2a1, and redis jumps to the 8.2.0b1 beta. No new regressions. The new observations are low-severity anomalies.
