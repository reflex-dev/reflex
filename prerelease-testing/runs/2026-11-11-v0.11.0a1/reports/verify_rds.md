Side anomaly, not filed (in-process microbenchmark only): two managers in one loop, where A runs 1000 back-to-back modifies with no gap. B's single waiter is starved for A's whole
loop on 0.11.0a1 (3.9-4.8 s, oplock on and off) and waits only 0.03-0.22 s on 0.10.1. The cause is `_wait_lock` "instaque" barging. A 3 ms gap removes it,
and the reporter's real-app matrix is fine. It is worth a follow-up look (`verify_rds/logs/barging.jsonl`).

Gaps in the repro notes: hard-coded paths, ports and drv venv name in the scripts; outage length not settable from `faults.sh`; the issue 1
mechanism is incomplete; the conc1 = 0/6 claim does not hold (1/6).
