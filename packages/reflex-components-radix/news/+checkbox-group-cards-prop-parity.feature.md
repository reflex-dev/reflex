`checkbox_group.root`/`item` and `checkbox_cards.root`/`item` now expose the same controlled-value and native form props already available on `radio_group`/`radio_cards`: `value`, `default_value` (cards only), `on_value_change`, `disabled`, `required`, `name` (cards only), `as_child`, `orientation`, `dir`, and `loop` on the roots, and `required` on the items. `checkbox_cards.item` also gains `value` and `disabled`, which were previously not settable at all. `checkbox_cards.root` is now marked as a form control, so its `name` is collected when a form validates its fields against a `TypedDict`-annotated `on_submit` handler, matching `checkbox_group`/`radio_cards`.
