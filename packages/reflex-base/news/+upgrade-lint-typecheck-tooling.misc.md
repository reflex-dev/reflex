Property docstrings are now noun phrases rather than "Get the ..." / "Return the ..." (ruff 0.16's new `D421`). `chain_updates()` declares its `events` parameter as `Any`, matching the runtime validation it delegates to. Internally, `ImportVar` and `unionize` are imported from the modules that define them instead of by way of `reflex_base.vars.base`, and two dead `is not None` guards were dropped from `_isinstance()` and the dependency-tracking bytecode scanner.
