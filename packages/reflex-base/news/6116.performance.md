`orjson_dumps` and `orjson_loads` in `reflex_base.utils.format` use orjson when installed, rendering byte-identical output either way. Socket payloads go through the new `orjson_dumps_socket`, which serializes with orjson unless the payload may contain a non-finite float: orjson emits `null` for those, so the stdlib takes over and `NaN`/`Infinity` reach the frontend intact.
