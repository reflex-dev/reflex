`orjson_dumps` and `orjson_loads` in `reflex_base.utils.format` use orjson when installed; `orjson_dumps` emits `null` for `NaN`/`Infinity`, so socket payloads go through the new `orjson_dumps_socket`, which round-trips them as sentinel strings the frontend restores. Without orjson, `orjson_dumps_socket` dumps once with the stdlib and only re-walks the payload when the output may collide with a sentinel.
