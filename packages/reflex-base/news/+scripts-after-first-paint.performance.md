Prerendered pages show up sooner: a production page no longer preloads its scripts ahead of the stylesheet it needs to paint, and starts them once it has painted. On a blank app, mobile Lighthouse LCP drops from about 2.9 s to 1.2 s (simulated slow 4G). Hydration starts later by the same design, and the window `load` event no longer waits for it. A click that comes before the page is hydrated starts the scripts right away and is dispatched again once its handler is attached; so does a hidden tab or a browser without paint timing, and a page that paints nothing starts them after one second. Dev mode and pages rendered in the browser are unchanged.
