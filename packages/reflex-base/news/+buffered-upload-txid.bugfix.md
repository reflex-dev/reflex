Events sent while a buffered upload (or another `EventProcessor.enqueue_stream_delta` stream) is in flight are no longer chained to it: an uploading client that disconnects no longer cancels other clients' events, the upload's response no longer waits for them, and navigating during an upload cancels the previous page's unfinished `on_load` handlers again. Concurrent buffered uploads no longer hang or end each other's responses early.
