Context providers no longer re-render their subtrees spuriously: `ThemeProvider` memoizes the `ThemeContext`/`ColorModeContext` values (and the `setColorMode`/`toggleColorMode` callbacks it hands out), and `EventLoopProvider` memoizes its provider element so router hook changes no longer cascade through the app. The `ThemeProvider` media-query listener is also attached once on mount instead of being torn down and re-added on every render.
