Component content hashing, which auto-memoization runs for every memoized component during a compile, is 2.3-2.6x faster on large pages. The hash now buffers its encoding instead of feeding the hasher one value at a time, dispatches on the exact type, and caches the encoded form of the strings and `ImportVar`s that recur across every component. Digests are byte-for-byte unchanged, so generated memo names stay stable.
