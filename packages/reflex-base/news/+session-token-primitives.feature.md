Add signed browser sessions and session-bound client tokens, with secret rotation through `REFLEX_SESSION_SECRET` and configurable session lifetimes. Signed session credentials are scoped to the app name, even when apps share signing keys. To move from the generated `.web/backend/session_secret` to `REFLEX_SESSION_SECRET` without ending existing sessions, list that file's contents after your new key, for example `REFLEX_SESSION_SECRET=<new key>,<old file contents>`.
