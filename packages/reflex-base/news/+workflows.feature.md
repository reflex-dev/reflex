`reflex_base.workflow` is the declaration half of Reflex Workflows: `WorkflowConfig`, the `durable=True` options on `@rx.event` (effect classes, `Retry`, timeouts, hooks), triggers (`manual`, `webhook` with HMAC and Stripe verifiers and rotating secrets, `schedule`), start policies (`Singleton`, `Debounce`, `RateLimit`, `Throttle`), `Signal` channels with correlated webhook triggers, and the control returns `after`, `wait_for`, `parallel`, `complete`, `fail`, and `needs_attention`. The runtime that executes them lives in `reflex.workflow`.
