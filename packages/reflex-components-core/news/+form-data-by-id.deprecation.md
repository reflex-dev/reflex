Submitting a form control's value under its `id` is deprecated and will be removed in 0.11; form data will then hold only what each control submits under its `name`. Compiling a form whose `on_submit` handler takes the form data now warns about each value-bearing control that has a static `id` but no `name`; give it a `name`, e.g. `rx.input(id="email")` becomes `rx.input(id="email", name="email")`. A control whose `id` differs from its `name` also stops adding a field keyed by its `id`, and a checkbox or switch whose `id` equals its `name` submits `"on"` or nothing instead of `True` or `False`; read it as a `bool` field of a `TypedDict` to keep a bool. See the [form docs](https://reflex.dev/docs/library/forms/form/) for details.
