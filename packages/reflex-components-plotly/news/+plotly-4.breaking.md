Upgrade Plotly.js and locales to 4.3. MathJax 2, Chart Studio options and source-reference attributes are removed. Mapbox figures keep rendering until Reflex 1.0, but Mapbox paths in buttons, sliders and templates no longer act, `on_relayout` data uses `map.*` keys, and Mapbox-hosted styles no longer load. See the [migration guide](https://reflex.dev/docs/library/graphing/other-charts/plotly/) for the changes to make.
