Deprecate `rx.plotly.mapbox` and Mapbox figures in Reflex 0.11.1 in favor of `rx.plotly.map` with MapLibre traces and `layout.map`. Until removal in Reflex 1.0, every Plotly component renders existing Mapbox figures on Plotly.js 4 by renaming their traces, `layout.mapbox` keys and subplot references, and logs a browser warning; styles hosted by Mapbox no longer load.
