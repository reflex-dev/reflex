Response models carry the fields the API returns that they previously dropped: `App` gains `backend_url`, `disable_secrets`, `weekly_report_enabled`, `source_thread_id`, `unreleased_provider` and the `any_environment_*` flags; `AppDeployment` gains `strategy`, `persistent` and `screenshot_uri`; `DeploymentRecord` gains `updated_by` and `promoted_from_id`; `ProjectAppDeployment` gains `updated_at`, `updated_by` and the `vm_type_*` fields; `Project` gains the `org_*` usage fields; `ProjectApp` gains `from_builder`; `ProjectMember` gains `role_permissions`; `AppSummary` gains `disable_secrets`; and `LogRecord` gains `event_id`, `stream_id` and `revision_id`.
