What starts each workflow is now one summary behind three surfaces: `reflex workflows triggers`, `GET /triggers` on the standalone service (read scope), and the console's Triggers page (start the console with your workflow module, `reflex workflows console workflows.py`, and it registers the definitions read-only). Each webhook shows its URL, whether it is signature-verified, and whether the verifier's secret is actually present in the environment; each schedule shows its cron, next occurrence, and how far its durable cursor lags — the "is my cron really registered, and what URL does this provider post to" questions answered without reading source.
