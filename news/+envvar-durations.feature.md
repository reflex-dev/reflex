The duration settings read by the app and the state managers take a unit suffix: `SQLALCHEMY_POOL_TIMEOUT`, `REFLEX_SOCKET_INTERVAL` and `REFLEX_SOCKET_TIMEOUT` accept values such as `2m`, and `REFLEX_AUTO_RELOAD_COOLDOWN`, `REFLEX_OPLOCK_HOLD_TIME` and `REFLEX_STATE_MANAGER_DISK_DEBOUNCE` replace the `_MS`/`_SECONDS` names, which still work with a deprecation warning until 2.0. A bare number is read as seconds.
