An app with `enable_state=False` no longer ships the backend transport or socket.io. Its root no longer publishes the bundled libraries on `window.__reflex`, which only dynamic components read, and those come from the backend; the namespace imports kept every export of `utils/state.js` and `utils/transport.js` in the bundle. In a small stateless app, the chunk every page loads shrinks from 93 KB to 12 KB (31 KB to 4.4 KB gzipped).
