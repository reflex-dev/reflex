The `refs` registry in `$/utils/state` is also published as a global, so a custom component's `add_hooks` or a raw `Var` that reads `refs[...]` without declaring the import keeps working as on 0.10, and code that declares its own `refs` shadows the global instead of conflicting with it. `refs` is internal and not covered by compatibility guarantees; act on a component by `id` with event actions such as `rx.set_value`, `rx.set_focus` and `rx.scroll_to`.
