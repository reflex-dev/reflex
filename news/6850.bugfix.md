Auto-memoized stateful components now forward runtime-injected props and refs to the component they wrap, so Radix `as_child`/`Slot` parents compose with stateful children: props injected by `rx.form.control(..., as_child=True)` (`name`, `id`, `aria-describedby`, a ref) reach the underlying input, and form fields bound to state are no longer missing from `on_submit`'s `form_data`.
