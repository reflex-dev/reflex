`rx.ModelRegistry.register(Base)` is now optional: until a base is registered, `reflex db` and the `alembic` command line migrate the tables of every `DeclarativeBase` subclass the app defines, so forgetting the line no longer makes `reflex db makemigrations` drop those tables and `reflex db migrate` delete their data. An app with a base that belongs to another database registers the bases of `db_url`, and an autogenerated revision that drops tables now logs a warning naming them.
