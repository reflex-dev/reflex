The migration logic moved out of `reflex.model` into the generated `alembic/env.py`: `reflex.model.alembic_autogenerate`, `reflex.model.get_migration_history`, `reflex.model.format_revision` and the matching `rx.Model.alembic_autogenerate` and `rx.Model.get_migration_history` class methods are removed. Use `alembic check`, `alembic revision --autogenerate` and `alembic history --indicate-current` instead; `reflex.model.migrate()` and `reflex.model.alembic_init()` remain.
