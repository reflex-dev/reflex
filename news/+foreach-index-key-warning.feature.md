`rx.foreach` now warns at compile time when an item with lifecycle events (`on_mount`, `on_unmount`) or hooks of its own is keyed by its index, which is the default. After an item is removed, its `on_unmount` runs for the item at the last position; set a unique, stable `key`, such as `key=item.id`, or `key=item` for a list of unique strings, on the component the render function returns.
