Webhook secrets rotate without a window of refusal. A verifier's secret variable — for `rx.hmac_signature` and `rx.stripe_signature` alike — may hold several comma-separated secrets, and a delivery verifies if it was signed with any of them. Rotation is three steps: list the new secret beside the old, cut the provider over, drop the old. Each candidate is compared in constant time; an unrelated secret never verifies, and dropping the old one ends its window immediately.
