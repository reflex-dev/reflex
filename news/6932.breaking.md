`transport="websocket"`, the default, now means a plain WebSocket rather than Socket.IO, and `python-socketio` and `uvicorn` moved to the `reflex[socketio]` and `reflex[uvicorn]` extras. Apps that pass `rx.App(sio=...)`, use `app.sio`, run uvicorn, proxy `/_event`, or use `AppHarness.backend` need changes: see [Upgrading to Reflex 0.10](https://reflex.dev/docs/changelog/upgrading/upgrading-to-0-10/#the-event-transport).
