Operator actions with no run to carry them — replaying a dead letter, purging finished runs — now land in an append-only audit log, written inside the operation's own transaction with who asked, what was done, to what, the outcome, and why. Read it with `reflex workflows audit`, `GET /audit` on the standalone service (read scope), or the console's Audit page. Only attributed actions are recorded, so automation leaves no entries and the log is operator decisions. Run-level actions keep riding each run's own history; this covers the rest.
