A prod app behind a TLS-terminating proxy or load balancer that keeps the `Host` header now accepts its own websocket whatever `X-Forwarded-Proto` says, including Caddy behind another TLS edge (the `docker-example/production` image on Render or Heroku), and setting `deploy_url` no longer rejects the backend's own origin. `X-Forwarded-Host` and `X-Forwarded-Proto` now count only when `trusted_proxy_hops` is set, so a proxy that rewrites `Host` and sends the original as `X-Forwarded-Host` (such as Azure Front Door) needs `REFLEX_TRUSTED_PROXY_HOPS=1` or `REFLEX_DEPLOY_URL=https://your.domain` on the backend. A rejected origin logs a warning naming the origin, what the backend saw, and the setting to change.
