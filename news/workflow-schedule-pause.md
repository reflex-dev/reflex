Schedules can be paused and resumed — `reflex workflows schedules pause KEY --reason ...`, `POST /schedules/{key}/pause` and `/resume` on the standalone service (operate scope), or the Pause/Resume buttons on the console's Triggers page. A paused schedule skips its occurrences while its durable cursor keeps moving, so resuming never backfills the pause: pause a nightly job for a week and you get one run on resume, not seven. Skipped occurrences are logged but do not count as lost work. Both actions are attributed and land in the audit log.
