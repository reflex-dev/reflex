Added internationalization support through the new `reflex-i18n` package, exposed under `rx.i18n`: translate static component content with `rx.t` and dynamic state content server-side with `rx.i18n.gettext`/`ngettext`/`pgettext`, and configure locales with `rx.i18n.I18nPlugin` in `rx.Config(plugins=[...])`. With `reflex-i18n` installed, `reflex i18n` manages the translation catalogs.
