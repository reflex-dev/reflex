Cancelling the parent of an all-mode `rx.parallel` fan-out no longer cancels its branches. Cancelling a parent tombstones its join, so when a branch later finished, its arrival found a slot that was no longer blocked — which the engine read as a race having been decided, and cancelled the sibling. Loser cancellation now runs only for a join that is actually a first-mode race and was not tombstoned, which is what the contract already promised: delegation is not ownership.
