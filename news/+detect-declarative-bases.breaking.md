`rx.ModelRegistry.register(Base)` is now optional: until a base is registered, `reflex db` and the `alembic` command line migrate the tables of every `DeclarativeBase` subclass defined at module level or in a class body of a module the app imports. A base meant for another database, or one defined by an installed library, then gets `create_table` in the `db_url` migrations; register the bases of `db_url` with `rx.ModelRegistry.register(Base)` to leave the others out. An autogenerated revision that drops tables now logs a warning naming them.
