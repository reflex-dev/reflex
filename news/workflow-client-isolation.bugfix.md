`rx.workflows.connect()` now binds the active client to a context variable rather than a process global, so two clients open at once — one per request, or one per tenant — can no longer route each other's runs into the wrong store. It also closes stores it opened itself, which stops a request-scoped client leaking a SQLite connection or a Postgres pool per request; a store passed in by the caller is left open, since it belongs to the caller.
