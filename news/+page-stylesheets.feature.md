`add_page` and `@rx.page` take `stylesheets=`, stylesheets only that page loads, after the app's: `app.add_page(pricing, stylesheets=["/pages/pricing.css"])`. They take the same asset files and URLs as `rx.App(stylesheets=...)` and become the page's React Router `links`, so they are in the prerendered `<head>`, load before the page renders on client-side navigation and are removed when it is left.
