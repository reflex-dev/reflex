Apps passed to `api_transformer` now get ASGI lifespan events, so the `lifespan` (and `on_startup`/`on_shutdown` handlers) of a FastAPI or Starlette transformer run, and state the lifespan yields is available on `request.state`. Events are forwarded through callable transformers as the ASGI spec describes; apps you mount on a transformer yourself still get none. If you worked around this by also registering the transformer's lifespan with `register_lifespan_task`, or by finding the transformer through the `starlette_app` lifespan task argument, remove the workaround: the first now runs the lifespan twice, and `starlette_app` is now the `Starlette` app serving Reflex's own routes.
