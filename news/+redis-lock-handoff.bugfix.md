Fixed the redis state manager stalling a token's events until its lock expired after an event was cancelled while taking the lock (for example by `supersedes`). A late release no longer deletes the next holder's lock. A lease now keeps its lock while the events queued ahead of its flush run, so their updates are no longer lost. Other instances waiting for the lock meanwhile stay registered, so its holder does not lease it again ahead of them. Waiters no longer wait out the whole lock expiration when the event ahead of them fails, is cancelled, or loses its lock, or when a release notification is lost.
