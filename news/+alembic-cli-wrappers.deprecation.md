`reflex.model.alembic_autogenerate`, `reflex.model.get_migration_history` and `reflex.model.format_revision`, and the `rx.Model.alembic_autogenerate` and `rx.Model.get_migration_history` class methods, are deprecated and will be removed in 1.0.0. They now run through the generated `alembic/env.py`, so `alembic_autogenerate` ignores its `connection` argument. Use `alembic revision --autogenerate`, `alembic check` and `alembic history --indicate-current`, or the matching `reflex db` commands, instead.
