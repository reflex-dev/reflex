Relative `url()`s in stylesheets from the assets directory, including shared assets that `rx.asset(shared=True)` links in from a package, resolve next to the stylesheet's real file: the build bundles the fonts and images they name instead of leaving URLs that only work at some depths. The styles directory links each stylesheet instead of copying it, falling back to a copy where symlinks are unavailable, and the dev server may serve files under the app root.
