Pages, memoized components and the app root import Emotion only when one of their components renders a `css` prop (a `style` on `rx.el.*`, `rx.box` and other Emotion-backed components, or an app-level `style`); every other generated module calls React's `createElement`. An app with no such style, such as one built from `rx.ui`, no longer ships Emotion: about 8 kB less gzipped JavaScript. Dynamic components load Emotion on demand when they need it.
